Objective: a repo derived from Lelemon-studio/compliance-cl (MIT) that covers every legal framework needed to operate with medical cannabis in Chile, starting with the sanitary pack, verified against official BCN texts.

- L5 2026-11-06 user (verbatim): "vamos resuelve". Ley 20.584 (idNorma 1039348, version 2026-02-16) Art. 12 defines the ficha clínica as kept by a prestador for the care it gave; Art. 3 defines prestador as whoever's activity is granting health care. Result: a record kept by an organization that gives no health care is not a ficha clínica; the pack resolves this with a two-question test, and leaves only the in-house non-prescribing professional case as `[criterio jurídico]`.
- L6 2026-11-06 Constitution (idNorma 242302, version 2026-04-16) verified: Art. 19 N° 1 (life and integrity), N° 9 (health; "deber preferente del Estado"), N° 15 (association without prior permission; associations contrary to public order prohibited). It does not mention collective cultivation: the pack records it as an argument (prescription → no state/private provision → association → cultivation) whose limit is Ley 20.500 Art. 1 "fines lícitos", CC Art. 559 c) 1 and the open Ley 20.000 question. Ley 21.663 Art. 4 lists "servicios digitales y servicios de tecnología de la información gestionados por terceros" as essential: it may reach the SaaS provider, not the OSFL. DL 825 Art. 2 N° 1: a sale is defined "independiente de la designación que le den las partes", so naming a payment "aporte" does not decide VAT.
